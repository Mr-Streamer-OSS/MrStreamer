Questions

More in the FAQ and the guides.

Does Mr. Streamer come with channels?

No. Mr. Streamer includes no channels and sells none. You connect the subscription you already have.

Does it work with M3U playlists and Xtream Codes?

Yes. Sign in with an Xtream Codes login or add an M3U playlist link. Live TV works straight away; for a playlist's movies and series, map its groups in Settings. Subscriptions

Is it really free?

Yes. It is open source under GPL-3.0, with no ads and no account.

Can I watch on my TV?

On a Mac, with AirPlay. On Windows, with Google Cast. On Linux it plays on the computer only. What plays
