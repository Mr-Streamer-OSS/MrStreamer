# Slice 4.5: movies and series like a streaming service

Status: built on 1 October 2026 on `t3code/movies-series-tabs`, in review. Slice 04 merged as #16 (`eca123f`) and shipped in nightly `0.0.3-nightly.20260930.37`; the update feed is live. [Known gaps](#known-gaps) lists what remains open.

Outcome: Movies and Series open faster, show each film once in the viewer's language, and are browsed through tabs of collections the app builds from TMDB's metadata instead of the provider's categories.

- What users see: [Movies and series](../../user/movies-and-series.md).

## Decisions

Wout's brief, after the slice 04 nightly: loading movies and series felt slow, the list of categories on the side felt unneeded, and the pages should take after Netflix, Hulu and Disney+ rather than mirror the provider's categories. Options were compared side by side outside the repository on 30 September and 1 October:

| Question             | Answer                                                                                                                                          |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Layout               | C: tabs under the top bar. For you, New, Genres, Services, 4K when the catalogue has any, and All movies or All series                          |
| Provider categories  | Not shown anywhere. The All tab replaces them                                                                                                   |
| Language             | English by default for everything, the viewer's choice remembered                                                                               |
| Slowest part         | The first open and load                                                                                                                         |
| Metadata             | Provider-agnostic: nothing may depend on a field only some providers send. TMDB, with a key built into the app and a field for the viewer's own |
| Attribution          | TMDB's logo and notice in About; JustWatch named where streaming services show                                                                  |
| Storage              | As little on the device as possible, artwork included, while staying smooth                                                                     |
| Release measurements | The Measure jobs go: they added about a quarter of an hour to every nightly and dry run. `compare-builds.ts` stays for runs by hand             |

Made while building:

- **One title per film.** Providers list each language version of a film as its own stream: "Speak No Evil (NL)", "(MULTI)", "(DE)". Versions sharing a TMDB id (`tmdb`, a standard Xtream Codes field) become one title; rows without one stay on their own. On Wout's provider that turns 53,422 movie rows into 35,720 titles and 10,439 series into 6,804. Details open on the version that suits the viewer best, and progress counts across versions.
- **Languages** come from the marks in names, a convention rather than a field (`@mrstreamer/core/ondemand/languages`). A version is in the viewer's language, in several, unmarked, or dubbed into another. Dutch, Flemish included, subtitles rather than dubs, so its mark says nothing about the sound to anyone else: "(NL)" on an English film is English with Dutch subtitles. Rows show titles in the viewer's language or several, and unmarked ones TMDB doesn't place in another language; All and search show everything.
- **Collections** are worked out in the catalogue worker from what every Xtream Codes provider sends (dates, names, ratings, 4K marks) and from TMDB (genres, original language, popularity, rating with its votes, streaming services). Nothing reads a category name.
- **Titles for adults** were only reachable through their own category. With categories gone, they are left out of every tab and of search. A setting could bring them back.
- **TMDB** answers in English, `en-US`, whatever the viewer's language: genres and collection names are the app's. It's asked about each listed title once, newest first, 8 requests at a time and at most 40 a second (TMDB allows about 50), and again after 150 days, inside its six-month limit. Streaming services come from TMDB's watch providers for the computer's region: the 12 most prominent per kind and their 5,000 most popular titles each, fetched weekly. A refused key stops the fetching, and Settings says so.
- **The built-in key** is a TMDB API Read Access Token, the repository secret `TMDB_API_KEY`, which the release workflow builds into the main bundle. Anyone can read it out of an installer. It only reads public data, and TMDB can revoke it; then a new release carries a new one, and meanwhile a viewer's own key in Settings takes over.
- **Storage:** the movie and series lists are packed with gzip, 17.7 MB down to 3.3 MB; TMDB's metadata is packed too. Chromium's disk cache, mostly artwork, is capped at 64 MB, and artwork is asked for at the size it's shown: 154, 342, 780 or 1,280 pixels wide from TMDB, which serves those sizes.

## Review

Found while building and fixed:

- English rows showed German dubs of English films: a title counted as suiting a language when TMDB said it was made in it, whatever its version was marked. Now only a mark that keeps the sound counts that way.
- zlib unpacked the cache in 16 KB chunks, which took 250 to 450 ms of the start; in 1 MB chunks it takes 30 to 70.
- The lists were written to disk before the first page answered, and the cache write could be lost when the worker stopped. Writes now wait a second after answering, and stopping waits up to 2 s for them.
- Home's All for new movies and series opened the page on whatever tab it was last on; it opens New this month now.

## Evidence

Checks at the final revision: `pnpm knip`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm fmt:check`, `pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm test` pass. The packaged-app test passes on a development build, opening its movie from the All movies tab.

The catalogue worker of `main` and of this branch, both bundled, against the real provider's lists served from the workbench, so only the app's own work counts. Eight rounds alternating which build goes first, with a metadata file the size the real catalogue fills (31,272 titles, 12 services per kind) for this branch. Each opens Movies as its build does: `main` the first page of every movie, this branch For you's rows.

| Measure, median and range                      | `main`                | This branch           |
| ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------- |
| Connecting: lists downloaded through to Movies | 2.52 s (2.20 to 2.99) | 1.77 s (1.48 to 2.11) |
| A start with the lists on disk, to Movies      | 1.03 s (0.87 to 1.17) | 1.29 s (1.10 to 1.32) |
| Lists on disk                                  | 17.7 MB               | 3.3 MB                |

A start is about 250 ms slower: it reads TMDB's metadata too, groups the versions and builds the collections, where `main` only indexed. Home asks for new movies and series as it starts, in both builds, so the worker has usually loaded by the time Movies opens. Most of a start goes to parsing the lists, working out display names and indexing 53,000 movies; keeping the worked-out names in the cache would save about a fifth.

Screens, driven in Electron against the real provider's lists with the fake TMDB: For you with a featured title and rows, New, Genres and a genre's grid with its sorts, Services, All movies, Series, Settings > Movies & series and About. With the language fix, English rows hold no titles marked for another language that dubs.

The real catalogue has 31,272 TMDB ids, movies and series together: at 40 a second, about 13 minutes for the first full fetch, newest first, plus a few minutes for streaming services.

## Known gaps

- Real TMDB is untested end to end: the workbench has no token, so its first run is the first nightly. Check on the Mac: genres and services fill in within minutes, backdrops show, Settings counts up, and About shows the logo.
- Mac measurements from the slice 04 nightly, before this slice, flagged cold start (1,535 and 1,598 ms against 1,125 and 1,004 ms for the nightly before) and memory with playback stopped (737 and 744 MB against 647 and 639 MB) on hosted runners. Linux showed +3 % and +23 MB. They need measuring again on the Mac mini; the catalogue worker's own engine and Home's poster rows are the likely causes.
- Titles without a TMDB id get no genres or services, so they show only in New, All and search.
- Nine languages to choose from. Marks outside the convention count as unmarked.
- The region for streaming services follows the system's; there is no setting.
- Titles for adults can't be reached at all.
- Changing the language indexes the catalogue again, about half a second.
