# iPhone setup

Mr. Streamer for iPhone requires iOS 16.4 or later. [Get it on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6823479796). It is free, with no ads, purchases or Mr. Streamer account. You need your own existing IPTV provider with Xtream Codes access; Mr. Streamer includes no channels and sells none.

## Connect your provider

1. Open Mr. Streamer and choose **Add provider**.
2. Choose **Don't share** or **Share** when asked about usage statistics. Both choices continue to setup. Statistics stay off until you choose to share them; you can change the choice in **Settings > Privacy**. An upgrade can retain an earlier deliberate choice. See the [privacy policy](../privacy.md).
3. Enter the server address, username and password supplied by your provider, then choose **Connect**. This is your provider login, not a new Mr. Streamer account.

## Watch and download

Live TV includes a Guide, now/next information and daily schedules when your provider supplies them. **Schedule** opens a channel's day; **Watch live** plays the channel. Movies and Series browse your provider's library. Download movies or episodes and open **Downloads** to play saved copies offline.

## Release scope

The first iPhone release is 0.1.0. It supports Xtream providers. M3U playlists, catch-up and Google Cast are later work. iPad and Apple TV are outside this release. The website's app pictures show the desktop app.

The other guides describe desktop controls, playback capabilities and installers. They do not establish iPhone feature parity. Update the iPhone app through the App Store; desktop Stable/Nightly installers and package-manager commands do not apply.
