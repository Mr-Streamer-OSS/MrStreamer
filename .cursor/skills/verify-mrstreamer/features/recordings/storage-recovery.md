# Interruptions, storage and lifecycle

Interruptions: a recording owes its time from its start to its stop. When capture stops early for the stream, the disk, the storage limits, the computer's sleep, a crash, or a system shutdown or session end, it reads **Paused** in Now with why and since when, and goes on with the rest of its time, appended to its file, once the app runs, the computer is awake, a connection is free and there is room. What passed meanwhile is a gap: its row says "20:31 to 20:45 missed." and why, its bar hatches it, and nothing fills it, from catch-up or otherwise. Rows count whole minutes, from the real captured and missed time added up first: captured rounds down, a complete one under a minute reading one, several gaps' missed total rounds once, and a gap, or gaps together, under a minute read "Less than a minute missed at 20:31." or "Less than a minute missed in between."; the bar draws each span at its real length. A gap begins where capture last wrote. Stop, on a recording or a paused one, and an explicit Quit the viewer confirmed end it for good; a stopped one never goes on. So does an HLS playlist that says its stream is over (`#EXT-X-ENDLIST`), and a provider that ends its answer cleanly within 30 seconds three times in a row, as with a short clip: both read Partial, the stream. One that reaches its stop with gaps reads Partial, its last gap closed at the stop. A live HLS channel going on after a gap shorter than its playlist's last three segments can repeat its first seconds: the card's known limit. A chosen folder that isn't there, as on a drive not plugged in, refuses a start rather than being made on another disk, and Settings' Open doesn't make it either. Choosing a folder the first time leaves `.mrstreamer-storage` in it, holding a random id, which stays; each recording made there keeps that id. A chosen folder without it, as the empty folder a drive's mount point keeps while the drive is away, counts as not there ("The folder for recordings isn't there. Connect its drive, or choose it again in Settings."): nothing starts there. A recording goes on only where its own id is, so it stays paused until its drive is back, also when that empty folder was chosen again meanwhile, which then takes only new recordings. One the app didn't see end is read as at a start only then, with its Stop or Quit mark and its file: it ends as it was marked, or goes on in its own file from its last write. Stopped, removed with its subscription or quit while its folder is away, it ends as recordings.json last had it, with what it held then and, after a crash, a gap from its start, which stays once the folder is back; such a Quit leaves no mark there, so a relaunch whose recordings.json refused it goes on with it. A copy of the folder keeping its mark passes for it. A mark is never written over, so a folder marked before marks held an id (only builds of this card's PR) keeps its empty one, and recordings made there go on wherever a mark is.

Storage: Settings, **Recordings** shows the folder new recordings go to with Change and Open, **Keep free on disk** (5 GB to begin with) and **Space for recordings** (No limit to begin with). At either limit a recording pauses, and goes on once there is room; a start with no room says so and asks the provider nothing. Nothing is deleted to make room: recordings stay until the viewer deletes them. Those made before a folder change stay where they were made.

Close, Quit and updates: closing the window never asks. While a recording's time goes on or a schedule is kept, the app keeps running without its window, and launching it again opens the window. macOS keeps the app as before; Windows and Linux show a tray icon with Open and Quit while the window is closed, and Ctrl+Q quits from the window. Only an explicit Quit asks first, while a recording's time goes on: **Quit while recording?** with Quit and Keep recording. Without a window, the app quits by itself once nothing records, no schedule is kept and no end waits to be written down. A shutdown never asks and leaves recordings to go on at the next start. A ready update with a recording live reads **Update after recording** and installs once nothing records, the release last asked for, with nothing new starting from then until the app quits or the install is refused; Restart now stops the recordings first.

Service contracts are in `apps/desktop/test/recordings.test.ts`: every track of the subtitles fixture recorded to its stop time and played through `openCopy`, Record now of the channel Watch plays reading Watch's own request (one provider request through Watch stopping, Watch joining again and the recording stopping, the last reader ending it, the recording and Watch's bytes decoding), busy without a provider request while Watch of another channel holds the one connection, **Stop playing and record** while the provider frees the stopped stream's slot late, and after a queued download took the connection it gave up, beside Watch with two connections, stop and broken-stream partials, a subscription removed while a start reads its M3U playlist again or turns active, quit then restart, the next stream after one with no program, a name within the file system's bytes, HLS segments with a refused separate-audio rendition, the HLS channel Watch plays recorded from the playlists and segments Watch reads behind a redirect to a tokened address (one provider request per segment through Watch stopping, joining again and the recording stopping, nothing asked once both left, the file decoding), a multivariant HLS channel recorded in the quality Watch plays, Watch asking for another meanwhile still read the held one and the provider sending only it, `playback.fixed` naming Watch's variant and then null as the recording stops, Watch joining a recording held to the recorded variant, and a 2160-line variant answering no `shares` and busy, another playlist channel at the same address answering no `shares` and `recording-holds` with no provider request, and Auto recording a channel's next stream when its playlist no longer names the first. Management contracts there: a broken stream paused and the rest of its time appended after a gap, decoding; a paused recording the viewer stopped never asked for again; sleep pausing and the next check going on; a shutdown's recording going on after the next start; a crash read as cut by the quit once its time is over, else paused since its file was last written, and a stray id folder removed; a crash as a start waits for its stream going on after the relaunch, its record written before its folder is made; an automatic end whose write failed listed as unsaved and written by the next check; played position kept across a restart, and a file removed outside the app refused before playback and listed missing at once; deleting a recording's saved subtitles and only its own; the space limit pausing and a raised limit going on; keep-free refusing a start with no provider request; a chosen folder recorded into and deleted from; a Stop marked in a chosen folder away at the relaunch kept once it is back; an empty folder in the chosen folder's place, also chosen again, after a shutdown while capturing, after a crash before the start made its folder, and put there while an M3U start reads its playlist again, recording nothing and refusing new starts, then going on in the returned folder's own file; Stop, Quit and Stop after an unwritten end of its own never going on after a relaunch whose recordings.json still said it records; starts held back for an update's restart only once nothing records; an update's install refused, with its reason, while a Stop is neither written nor marked, also on one that ended by itself, and installed after Try again once storage works, that Stop kept after a relaunch; a failed start's record taken out of `recordings.json` by the next check once storage works, and never recorded after a Quit and relaunch while only `recordings.json` was refused, also from a chosen folder unreadable, absent or replaced by an empty one as the app started; an unreadable `storage.json` left as it is, refusing starts and changes; and a refused personal-position write retaining its last saved value and succeeding on retry while `schedules.json` can't be read.

An end that couldn't be written down is listed with **Not saved yet. Trying again.**, and the schedule check writes it every 10 seconds until storage works; Stop also retries it, never silently succeeding: its row moves to Recorded and says the same, not the Stop's save error, and drops it once the check saves the end, in the same window. A Stop refused while the recording goes on, and a refused Delete, say why in place of the reason. Until it is written, a relaunch reads it as one the app didn't see end: paused when its time remains, else Partial cut by the quit with the file's actual size. Stop, also on one that ended by itself, a subscription's removal and Restart now, all as stopped, and a confirmed Quit, as the quit, also for one whose own end wasn't written, leave, while its time remains, an empty `.mrstreamer-stopped` or `.mrstreamer-quit` in the recording's folder, so a relaunch ends that one as Stopped or Partial, the quit, as of then, never going on. Only a disk that refuses both writes, as one gone read-only, loses that: the Quit doesn't wait for it. An update's restart does not go ahead then: main writes both again just before it installs, and while a stopped recording is kept by neither, Settings shows the release couldn't be installed, **A recording you stopped couldn't be saved as stopped. Check its storage, then try again.**, with Try again, also after the window was reloaded. Writes of `recordings.json` are tried again while `schedules.json` can't be read, too. An unreadable `storage.json` is never written over: Settings, Recordings says it can't be read, and new recordings refuse to start while paused ones go on where they began, under the default limits (5 GB kept free, no space limit).
