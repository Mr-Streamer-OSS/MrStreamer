# Scheduling controls and contracts

Schedules, from a programme's details in the guide grid: **Record** opens a panel under the details with **Repeat** (**Once**, the programme's weekday or a repeat's own days, **Every day**), **Start** and **End** padding steppers (0 to 60 min, 2 early and 5 late to begin with, within the 12 hours a recording may run; a repeat of a longer programme records 12 hours), a line saying when it records and whether a connection is free then, or **Every connection is in use then.** with what holds it and **Schedule anyway**, and **Schedule**. Once is the guide programme at its start, whose end follows the guide; a repeat is a channel and a local time of day, whatever is on. A scheduled programme's cell has a ring, and its details read **Scheduled**, which edits it or cancels it. Recordings lists **Scheduled** between Now and Recorded: title, channel and padded times, the repeat, its next time and how it stands (a connection is free, in use and by what, waiting for one, recording, the guide lists another programme or none then), **Edit** in a popover and a menu with **Cancel schedule**. A schedule records through the same capture and admission as Record: at its padded start, or late with the rest of its time when the app was closed, the computer slept or every connection was held, and a row says it started late and why, its bar hatching what it missed, counted from when the schedule was saved for one saved partway through its time. With `recordings.json` unreadable, nothing is scheduled either. A time that passed with nothing captured is a **Missed** row with why and **Remove**. Nothing stops for a schedule, and it never wakes or starts the computer.

From 0.0.24, programme-following rules are described in the [Recordings feature entry](../recordings.md), with their planner, identity and capacity limits. A refused startup rule inventory stays refused until the next start; the page explains that a restart reloads it, while ordinary recording-read failures keep Retry.

## Gotchas

A schedule whose time comes while every connection is held asks again every 10 seconds, and when a recording ends; one whose start failed asks again about once a minute. Nothing playing or downloading stops for it. Pending schedules on one subscription start one at a time, by higher priority first, then earlier padded start, earlier creation time and finally id for deterministic ties. This order never preempts active viewers or recordings. A programme schedule waits for its guide to be read before it starts, so it never records another show.

Schedules are kept in `schedules.json` beside `recordings.json`, which a build from before them leaves alone. Such a build refuses a `recordings.json` holding a missed result, leaving it as it is and recording nothing, as with any file it can't read; no released build includes Record now.
