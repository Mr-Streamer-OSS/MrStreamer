# Policy and acceptance limits

## Limits

`docs/privacy.md` holds the published policy, website analytics and attributed provider retention terms. Its current desktop event list and the Settings note describe the app's events; before release, check them against `docs/telemetry.md`. Loopback payloads prove the integration contract; live ingestion, dashboard counts and server deletion deadlines remain unverified. `apps/desktop/test/analytics.test.ts` checks the owner's public contract: exact local HTTP payloads per event, consent generations for transactions and operations, update-channel stamping, quit flushing of the current run's end-only summary, rate limiting and the 64-event outbox, retries under the same UUID with preserved pending backoff across preference saves and stale-send rejection after consent ABA, format-one migration that discards queued summaries, and opt-out during startup. Its playback cases cover preview eligibility, incident deduplication and recovery, stale bindings and sequences, renderer loss with remote continuation, leases and suspend, consent ABA without historical starts or stops, receiver load failures before and after Output's observer, replay controls and normal-admission fallback. `analytics-open.test.ts` checks that only a current open's own failure reports a fixed code. Receiver terminal ends are checked at the owner (with and without a renderer, against replaced loads, output moves, older runs and opt-out), through a fake Cast receiver in `output.test.ts` (played to its end, Stop, stopped on the TV, release and a broken channel stream) and in the renderer controllers in `renderer/receiver.test.ts`. Public transaction tests check shared refresh, source no-ops, favourite variants/receipts and reorder exclusion, effective preference no-ops and current-target update dismissal. A fake Cast receiver drives generation-guarded main Output observations; it does not establish real AirPlay/Cast hardware acceptance. Renderer contracts check unpaused progress and seek/wait/pause observations without transmitting media positions. Native checks for the exact installed candidate stay on the release card. Real-provider playback uses the separate authorized acceptance workflow. `analytics-summaries.test.ts` covers five-minute counted checkpoint writes and explicit pause/stop with additional watch time, a durable zero-time first checkpoint and no waiting/seeking or paused-scrub writes, crash snapshot/restart with stable UUID/original envelope/no dead time, independent consent reads during recovery, optional-block isolation and per-item mixed expiry over actual HTTP, separate admission, all durable items within 64 with both open owners retained, idle retirement of exhausted direct reservations without deleting an active third delivery, and real HTTP body-timeout deliveries capped at three, refused-connect retention, expiry and the 1.5-second fail-closed checkpoint deadline. Interrupted watch duration can lose five minutes plus the lease, and no return means no report. Native installed kill/upgrade/offline and Windows rename/checkpoint latency remain release-owner checks. Saved-report migration precedes merge, and first-nightly reconciliation precedes stable.

Actual renderer exits must be observed from main; main crashes and power loss cannot be claimed from these events.
