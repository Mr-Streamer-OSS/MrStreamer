# iOS subtitles and language

On iOS, online subtitles use SubDL only. Settings > Subtitles saves the viewer's own key in the Keychain, shown as Saved or Not set, and a text size. In Watch, a movie's or episode's **Subtitles** sheet lists the file's tracks and Off, then the result kept for that exact version, **Find online** and, while one shows, **Adjust timing** (0.1 and 1 second steps, frame-rate conversions between 23.976, 24 and 25 fps, Reset). Entry points: `apps/ios/src/watch.tsx` (`subtitleRows`, `Cues`), `apps/ios/src/online-subtitles.ts` (search and download), `apps/ios/src/downloaded-subtitles.ts` (per-version keeping, play binding, timing) and the playback owner's Off override in `apps/ios/src/playback.ts`. `apps/ios/test/online-subtitles.test.ts` drives the real owners against a SubDL stand-in: exact request parameters with no provider data, a title-only answer kept only for the name and year asked, failure reasons including a withheld Keychain key, ZIP, host and redirect refusals (a file elsewhere dropped, the others still listed), a search outlived by another play, a download outlived by a file-track pick or an erasure, a refused save, restore for the next play, the file's own subtitles off without changing the preference, and erasure by subscription. `playback-requests.test.ts` checks the identity real movie and episode plays carry, and `downloads.test.ts` a real copy's play showing its version's subtitle with no request, asking by its movie's or series' TMDB id (by name for a copy saved before ids were kept). `online-subtitles-erasure.test.ts` erases through the real account's history removal and Demo reset; `watch-subtitles.test.ts` drives Watch's Find online, the results sheet's notice and the cue overlay; `media-settings.test.ts` drives the key and Text size rows and sees no SubDL request from them. Known unsupported output: while a downloaded subtitle is on, Picture in Picture and AirPlay show no subtitles at all (Watch's sheets say so where it is chosen). Without a native slot, the cue overlay's drawing and timing against AVPlayer, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver in the sheets, Hermes `TextDecoder` with `fatal`, streaming `fetch` bodies, redirects as iOS follows them, and real SubDL answers remain unproven.

On iOS, **Settings > General > Interface language** also redraws Watch and its sheets, the mini player, Home, Search, Movies/Series, details, Watchlist, Downloads and Settings' own pages in English, Dutch, French, German or Spanish. `apps/ios/src/i18n/watch.ts`, `library.ts`, `downloads.ts` and `pages.ts` provide typed messages. Provider/catalogue names and track language facts remain unchanged; accessibility action identities stay stable while their labels translate, and the shared Diagnostics report stays English for support. A switch preserves the player, stream, position, speed, tracks, browse filters/version/season, Search query/results and download queue. Open series details read their facts again, so their season headings follow the language (see Season labels below). Known saved download reasons are translated when displayed, including earlier translated network reasons. Existing playback failures, open system sheets/alerts, an in-progress series summary and a download fenced because its title disappeared retain their recorded wording.

`apps/ios/test/watch-language.test.ts`, `library-language.test.ts`, `downloads-language.test.ts`, `pages-language.test.ts`, the language case in `search-screen.test.ts` and `live-quality.test.ts` cover public redraw/state and action behavior; red/green checks cover the version line, numbered season headings, legacy download reasons and Automatic. Two iPhone Simulator runs observed representative journeys in all five languages, French/German large text and retained playback/browse state. A historical follow-up on `c7c02e33`, before provider season names gave way, exercised the production cached-details path: French Saison 2 became selected German Staffel 2 and the source version stayed. Live Quality’s Automatic label was observed in German, French and Dutch. Specials remains test-only because the native fixture has no Specials season. SubDL results/timing, Playlist groups, iPad, hardware shortcuts, VoiceOver speech/focus/action delivery and physical devices remain separate acceptance; accessibility snapshots prove labels only.

Season labels: both apps name every numbered season by its number in the interface language (Season 2, Staffel 2) and season 0 Specials (Épisodes spéciaux, Especiales), whatever the provider calls it. `seriesDetails` in `packages/core/src/ondemand/details.ts` makes the names; the provider's own season names stay in its details, unshown, and its numbers still group, match and play episodes. The fake provider's Dutch Seizoen 1 shows as Season 1 in English. `packages/core/test/ondemand.test.ts` covers the five catalogues with foreign provider names and Specials, `apps/desktop/test/ondemand.test.ts` the desktop service reading details again after a language change, `renderer/details-back.test.ts` a desktop switch in Settings keeping the season chosen and its episodes, and `apps/ios/test/library-language.test.ts` an iOS switch keeping them.
