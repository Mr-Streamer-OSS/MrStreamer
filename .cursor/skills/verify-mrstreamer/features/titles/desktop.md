# Desktop library and title baseline

## Sub-features

Movie and series libraries, search, quality/language filters, metadata and episode details, version choice, playback/resume, file tracks, online subtitles with saved timing, and marking episodes watched.

Opening a title or offline copy claims the shared media element and ends the previous live playback. Explicit live playback closes the title and cancels even a countdown held by Settings; preview and stale live retries cannot replace it. Same-title recovery, next episode, paused engine release and receiver following retain title ownership until close. Public handoff/adoption/late-preview cases are in `renderer/playback-admission.test.ts`; held-countdown handoff is in `renderer/next-episode.test.ts`. Built-app and native handoff checks follow the Live TV feature entry, alongside downloads' provider-preemption and offline paths.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open **Movies** or **Series** in the header. **All movies** and **All series** include every allowed title; other tabs filter and group the catalogue. The page's search is separate from global search. A poster opens details with Play/Resume, versions and Save. Series details show seasons and episodes. The compact Sort, Quality, Language and Verified menus filter the current grid or page search; global search stays unfiltered. Each menu lists every available choice with its current selection checked. Any clears that filter. Long menus scroll, and Escape returns focus to their trigger. With the menu closed, Escape leaves the open collection; application modifier shortcuts pass through the controls. Provider hint descriptions and locally verified-file counts are stated inside their menus, including zero verified files. Collection counts show the filtered and original totals beside the collection name. Sort remains local to the collection.

Details show **Also in your subscriptions**, related titles from every saved subscription's loaded lists, never titles for adults; an adult title's own row is empty. A related title opens its details in place, and **Back** in the top bar the sheet keeps above its backdrop returns through those it was opened from, at most eight, with the season picked, the scroll and the keyboard on the related title opened. With none left, Back returns to the page, as Close and Escape do at once; the keyboard then returns to the control that opened the first details, when it is still on the page. A title opened from a page, search or the player starts a new way back. Playing a title or opening Settings over the details keeps it, and details Settings closed reopen on the season picked (`heldSeason` in the same store), as after a change of interface language. Removing a subscription takes its titles out of it and closes details of its own; hiding titles for adults and removing the last subscription close it all. Back asks the lists first: a title they have since marked for adults while Settings hides them is passed over and never shows. Each visit is a sheet of its own, so Back to an earlier visit of the same title restores it at once. Search opened over details, from their bar or with ⌘K or Ctrl K, gives the keyboard back to what had it. The trail lives in `app/ui-store.ts` (`openRelated`, `backDetails`, `closeDetails`) for the session only.

## Driving it with Electron CDP

Preconditions: built app with fresh fixture provider/TMDB. Run `pnpm verify:desktop titles`. Open Movies > All movies > the fixture poster whose title includes `Two sound tracks`, then close details and open Series > All series > a poster containing `Formats`. Require actual detail dialogs, Save buttons, at least two fake-TMDB detail requests and retained dialog screenshots. This baseline verifies browsing/details, not playback or every filter.

## Gotchas

TMDB enrichment changes displayed names; fixture poster titles still include provider names. A populated grid is not proof of playback, filters or version choice. Adult content needs its explicit Settings switch. Once it is off, Movies and Series offer no Adults tab or its titles, including at first paint after the switch and from an answer read while it was on; `renderer/adult-visibility.test.ts` holds those answers. In the built app, turn it on, read Movies > Adults, go Home, turn it off and open Movies with a DOM observer. Some verified series tracks only become filterable after reopening their details; check current user docs before treating absence as a defect.
