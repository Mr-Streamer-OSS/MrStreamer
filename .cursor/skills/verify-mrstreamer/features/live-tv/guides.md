# Guide sources and labels

A subscription's channels read its own guide, then the XMLTV sources of Settings > Guides in their order; the first guide with programmes for a channel supplies all of it, and a mapping by hand comes first. Each source is downloaded and indexed once for every subscription. Run `pnpm verify:desktop guides` (source `scripts/guides.ts`): two fake guide hosts, North and South, each list a channel of `TEST | Formats and failures` the other doesn't and one both do, under guide ids the provider's own guide lacks. It adds both through the field and **Add**, and pass means each row is seen downloading, South's with how much of how much and its still bar, then reading and ready with what it supplies; each host was asked once; Live TV shows the provider's own programmes on `H.264 + AAC`, North's and South's on theirs and North's on the shared one; after **Move … up** on South the shared one shows South's; North failing with HTTP 503 on its refresh turns its row red with why and since when while its programmes stay in use; and the subscription's Guide row counts what each guide supplies. Retained: each state's screenshot at 1280 px, the failed row, the Guide row and Live TV at 1280 and 900, and every line each row showed in `proof.json` (`northStates`, `southStates`).

A source goes by the name the viewer gave it, at Add or with the row's pencil, else its host, with the start of its random id where two would read alike (`renderer/src/lib/guide-names.ts`); the row, each of its buttons, the subscription's Guide row and the mapping sheet use that name. Run `pnpm verify:desktop guide-labels` (source `scripts/guide-labels.ts`): one fake guide host serves `/a.xml` and `/b.xml` behind its key. Pass means both added unnamed read as the host with a tag; Enter names A "Netherlands" and hands the keyboard back to its pencil; naming B "netherlands" by leaving the field tags both; Escape keeps that; Enter names B "United Kingdom" and its second line leads with the host; every row and button name differs at each step; Move United Kingdom up keeps the keyboard on its other arrow; the Guide row says "1 United Kingdom · 1 Netherlands" and the mapping sheet "United Kingdom · b.src"; a restart keeps names and order; B failing with HTTP 503 shows Retry United Kingdom; the buttons read in en, nl, de, fr and es at 1280 and 900; clearing A's name shows the host; removing United Kingdom puts the keyboard on the host's Remove; B added again with a name from the form is named; and neither path nor key is in the page text, attributes, accessibility tree or retained evidence. `guide-source.test.ts` ("a source's name") holds names through moves, refreshes, failures, restarts and a locked address typed again, the trimming, length cap and the refusal of `://`; `renderer/guides-section.test.ts`, `guide-names.test.ts`, `guide-map.test.ts` and `subscription-section.test.ts` hold the window's side. 0.0.13 keeps the names it doesn't know when it rewrites `sources.json` (checked once against the official 0.0.13 AppImage, not a maintained scenario).

Guides are indexed on disk in a worker thread, one SQLite file per document, and `guide.status` and `guide.sources` tell each step in `processing` (downloading, reading, ready, failed). After a change to guide storage or indexing, run `guide-index.test.ts`, `guide.test.ts` and `guide-source.test.ts` (status steps, the guide before answering meanwhile, a grid's stretch with retained past and a slot's description from the guide channel it came from, a guide of large descriptions in a small worker heap, sources in order and shared by subscriptions, a profile kept by 0.0.12 or with a subscription's own address), drive `pnpm verify:desktop live-tv`, `guides` and `guide-source.ts` on a fresh build for listings and search in the window, and measure with `measure-guide.ts`, also `--file` on a large real guide kept in `.local/`, and with `--source` as one source of Settings > Guides ([guide budgets](../../../../../docs/contributing/testing.md#guide-budgets)).
