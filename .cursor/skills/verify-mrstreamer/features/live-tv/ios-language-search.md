# iOS language, programme search and reminders

On iOS, **Settings > General > Interface language** changes Live, Find countries or categories, Favourites, custom lists, Guide/Schedule, programme details, Reminders and their notification words between English, Dutch, French, German and Spanish. The shared status line follows the same choice on Home, Live, Movies, Series and Search; the rest of those surfaces has its own translation scope. `apps/ios/src/i18n/live.ts` and `i18n/status.ts` supply typed messages, with day/time/count/provider-list formatting in the interface language. Provider, channel, category, list and programme names stay as supplied; country names remain the catalogue's English keys. Open rows and memoised cells subscribe to the language, and `reminders.ts` schedules existing notifications again in the new words. `apps/ios/test/live-language.test.ts`, `reminders.test.ts`, `loading-feedback.test.ts` and `search-screen.test.ts` cover portable redraw, retained list/country context, notification rescheduling and a single translated Retry. For native proof, use the ordinary language setting with fake services, check representative screens in all five languages, narrow French/German text at large sizes, and retained selection, draft and scroll context across a switch. Change the language several times in one session without relaunching and check the tab bar, the tab buttons' spoken labels in an accessibility snapshot, and the back button of Settings opened from Live, Movies or Series after each change (Home's root has no title, so its back button is the system's). The visible labels and the back button refresh through the react-native-screens patch (`apps/ios/README.md`), the spoken labels through `(tabs)/_layout.tsx`, so a JS test with mocked tabs cannot show them. Spoken labels in test stand-ins do not establish VoiceOver, and rescheduling tests do not establish delivered notification text; record those and iPad split-column acceptance separately.

On iOS, Search's **Programmes** section finds guide programmes on now or later whose title has every word of the query, from the provider's guide and external XMLTV guides (`guide.ts` `search`, native `GuideIndex.search`). It lists at most 20, on now first, each on the first listed channel that reads its programmes from the guide and id it was found by, as Live's now and next read them, with its day when not today and the provider where several are signed in. A row opens the programme's detail inside Search without playing; **Back to Search** returns to the same query and results. The iOS swipe-back gesture still closes Search from the detail instead of returning to results. A guide that publishes, a replaced or removed provider, new mappings and a stale guide change what it finds, and an answer held across them never shows. An open detail closes when its provider login or requested channel catalogue changes. An ordinary guide reread keeps the detail mounted and uses a replacement token when the programme is returned again. Outside the bounded results, the retained token remains readable only while it is valid; once withdrawn without a replacement, the detail says "Programme unavailable. Go Back and choose it again." A guide that could not be searched in the foreground adds **Some programmes not searched.** to the status line, the other guides' programmes still show, and Search no longer claims nothing has the name. `apps/ios/test/guide.test.ts` and `search-screen.test.ts` cover portable behavior, and `apps/ios/modules/guide/test/main.swift` the native search. The native search, its speed on a large guide, scroll restoration after **Back to Search**, VoiceOver and layouts still need device acceptance.

On iOS, programme reminders (`apps/ios/src/reminders.ts` over core's reminder rules, `reminders-screen.tsx`, native `apps/ios/modules/programme-reminders`) are iOS local notifications scheduled at each programme's start. A programme still to come offers **Remind me** after **Watch live**; iOS asks once, a refusal saves nothing and offers **Open iOS Settings**, and a set one shows a bell after its time and **Cancel reminder**. Live's reminders list (`live/reminders`) groups them by day, says "On now", "Changed to … · won't notify" or "No longer in the guide · won't notify", and cancels with a cross, a swipe to the left or VoiceOver's action. A 65th reminder to notify, past the 64 iOS holds, is refused, as is one iOS won't schedule or allow; one a guide brings back past the 64, or iOS refuses later, says "Not scheduled by iOS · won't notify" and shows no bell. Checks at launch, return to the app and provider, channel or guide changes take a notification down when another programme or none starts then, also when that finding can't be written, and restore it when the programme returns; a guide that can't be read changes nothing. While iOS doesn't allow notifications none stays scheduled, and a reminder that goes takes its shown notification down too. The notification reads the programme's title over "Starting now on {channel}". A tapped one opens the programme's details on Live by channel and start (`ProgrammeScreen`'s `start`, `guide.ts` `programmeAt`), in Live's detail column from the split width, and plays nothing. `apps/ios/test/reminders.test.ts` covers this through the real files stand-in, guide owner and screens, with iOS's notification centre faked. Permission prompts, delivery while the app is closed, the foreground banner, a tap that launches the app, the lock screen, VoiceOver and layouts need a native run.
