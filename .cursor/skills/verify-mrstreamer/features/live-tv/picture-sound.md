# Picture and sound adjustments (desktop 0.0.24 development)

One set of adjustments for everything that plays on this computer: sound delay (0 to 2000 ms, sound after the picture only), opt-in leveling, aspect (as the stream says, 16:9, 4:3, 21:9), crop to one of those ratios, zoom (100 to 300 %) and Reset. They live in the active profile's `playbackAdjustments` preference (`packages/contracts/src/preferences.ts`) and go through `preferences.update`; a receiver plays the stream as sent.

- `apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/features/watch/PictureSound.tsx` is the panel: Sound delay (− and + in 50 ms steps), Leveling Off/On, Aspect, Crop, Zoom (25 % steps), Reset, the failure line with Retry, and on a receiver only its refusal line. A title opens it from the bar's **Picture and sound** button (pressed while anything differs from the defaults); Live TV from **More > Picture and sound** (`LiveMore.tsx`, which says "Adjusted" beside it). Up and Down move between rows, Left and Right pick within Leveling, Aspect and Crop, End reaches Reset.
- `apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/player/adjustments.ts` holds what plays: `adjustPlayback`, `resetPlayback`, `retryPlayback`, `usePlaybackAdjustments` (adjustments, saving, failed with its message) and `useAdjustmentsRefusal` for a receiver. A failed save plays the saved adjustments again and keeps the failed choice for Retry.
- `apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/player/sound.ts` is the one sound chain. Both `engine.ts` (every live engine, through its shared lifecycle) and `title-engine.ts` (titles and downloads) attach their element. Default sound never touches Web Audio. The first delay or leveling routes attached elements through one `AudioContext`: media element source, delay, two compressors, gain. An element stays routed for the window's life, passing sound unchanged at the defaults. The native engine asks for CORS, which the loopback proxy grants, so Web Audio hears the element.
- `apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/player/picture-fit.ts` gives the picture's box; `Picture.tsx` applies it only for `adjusted` pictures: Watch and the title player, both full and mini. Previews and multiview panes stay fitted.

Contract tests: `apps/desktop/test/renderer/playback-adjustments.test.ts` (failed save, Retry, Reset, picture geometry) and `apps/desktop/test/renderer/picture-sound.test.ts` (the panel: steps and picks saved within the limits, Reset, the failure line and Retry, the receiver line).

Built-app journey: build, run doctor, then `xvfb-run -a pnpm verify:desktop picture-sound` (source `scripts/picture-sound.ts`). Constant-rate fake live encodes (AAC direct, MP2 converted) and the fake title. It plays the direct channel as the stream comes and requires no Web Audio context. Then, in **More > Picture and sound** while the channel plays, it steps Sound delay to 200 ms, picks Leveling On and Crop 4:3, steps Zoom to 150 %, and requires the picture to move at once. After a restart, which records the saved `playbackAdjustments`, it plays the direct channel, the converted channel, the two-track title, a switch to its Spanish track and the downloaded copy with Watch offline. In that copy's **Picture and sound** menu it presses End and Enter to Reset, requires the picture to fill its area again and the saved adjustments to be the defaults. Each stage requires a box matching `pictureBox` within a pixel, six media seconds keeping wall pace, and, over CDP `WebAudio`, one realtime context with one routed element and its render capacity. A third start after Reset requires no context again. Last, the window renders synthetic signals through the bundled `sound.ts` chain offline: measured delay against each setting, with and without leveling; peak and full-scale samples of quiet, full-scale and burst passages; channels kept from a six-channel input; render speed.

Recorded on Linux Xvfb with muted output (12 November 2026), before the panel existed, with the adjustments written to `preferences.json` between starts; the UI-driven journey above has not run yet: delay exact to the sample; leveling adds 12 ms, peaks at 0.913 with no full-scale samples where the input had 96,160, and narrows 40 dB between quiet and loud to 26.5 dB. Delay keeps six channels and leveling mixes to two. Leveling renders at about 240 times real time offline and takes 0.5 to 1.2 % render capacity while playing.

Not covered here: aspect in the built-app journey (the picture-fit contract test covers it), audible output, native macOS or Windows, Bluetooth or surround devices, the extra output latency of the Web Audio route against Chromium's own, and receivers. Sound earlier than the picture is not offered: an element plays its sound and picture on one clock.
