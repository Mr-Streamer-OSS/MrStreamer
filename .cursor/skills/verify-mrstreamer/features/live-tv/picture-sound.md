# Picture and sound adjustments (desktop 0.0.24 development)

One set of adjustments for everything that plays on this computer: sound delay (0 to 2000 ms, sound after the picture only), opt-in leveling, aspect (as the stream says, 16:9, 4:3, 21:9), crop to one of those ratios, zoom (100 to 300 %) and Reset. They live in the active profile's `playbackAdjustments` preference (`packages/contracts/src/preferences.ts`) and go through `preferences.update`; a receiver plays the stream as sent.

- `apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/player/adjustments.ts` holds what plays: `adjustPlayback`, `resetPlayback`, `retryPlayback`, `usePlaybackAdjustments` (adjustments, saving, failed with its message) and `useAdjustmentsRefusal` for a receiver. A failed save plays the saved adjustments again and keeps the failed choice for Retry.
- `apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/player/sound.ts` is the one sound chain. Both `engine.ts` (every live engine, through its shared lifecycle) and `title-engine.ts` (titles and downloads) attach their element. Default sound never touches Web Audio. The first delay or leveling routes attached elements through one `AudioContext`: media element source, delay, two compressors, gain. An element stays routed for the window's life, passing sound unchanged at the defaults. The native engine asks for CORS, which the loopback proxy grants, so Web Audio hears the element.
- `apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/player/picture-fit.ts` gives the picture's box; `Picture.tsx` applies it only for `adjusted` pictures: Watch and the title player, both full and mini. Previews and multiview panes stay fitted.

Contract tests: `apps/desktop/test/renderer/playback-adjustments.test.ts` (failed save, Retry, Reset, picture geometry).

Built-app journey: build, run doctor, then `xvfb-run -a pnpm verify:desktop picture-sound` (source `scripts/picture-sound.ts`). Constant-rate fake live encodes (AAC direct, MP2 converted) and the fake title. It plays the direct channel as the stream comes and requires no Web Audio context. It then saves 200 ms, leveling, 4:3 crop and 150 % zoom to `preferences.json` and restarts. Through the UI it plays the direct channel, the converted channel, the two-track title, a switch to its Spanish track and the downloaded copy with Watch offline. Each stage requires a box matching `pictureBox` within a pixel, six media seconds keeping wall pace, and, over CDP `WebAudio`, one realtime context with one routed element and its render capacity. A third start with `DEFAULT_PLAYBACK_ADJUSTMENTS` saved requires no context again. Last, the window renders synthetic signals through the bundled `sound.ts` chain offline: measured delay against each setting, with and without leveling; peak and full-scale samples of quiet, full-scale and burst passages; channels kept from a six-channel input; render speed.

Recorded on Linux Xvfb with muted output (12 November 2026): delay exact to the sample; leveling adds 12 ms, peaks at 0.913 with no full-scale samples where the input had 96,160, and narrows 40 dB between quiet and loud to 26.5 dB. Delay keeps six channels and leveling mixes to two. Leveling renders at about 240 times real time offline and takes 0.5 to 1.2 % render capacity while playing.

Not covered here: the player controls that set the adjustments (designer slice), audible output, native macOS or Windows, Bluetooth or surround devices, the extra output latency of the Web Audio route against Chromium's own, and receivers. Sound earlier than the picture is not offered: an element plays its sound and picture on one clock.
