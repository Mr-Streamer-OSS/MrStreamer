# Desktop Live TV baseline

Live and title playback share one media claim. An explicit channel, archive or zap closes the title immediately; preview, retry, quality and reconnect work cannot displace an open title. A delayed preview read cannot open an older channel after another selection. Preview to Watch keeps the same provider session. Main still owns actual connection admission and receiver transport. `renderer/playback-admission.test.ts`, `receiver.test.ts` and `next-episode.test.ts` cover both handoff directions, adoption without a receiver Stop, obsolete preview reads and cancellation of a held countdown. On a built app, cover preview to movie, return to preview, episode end under Settings, then a channel from global Search; require one provider reader and no later automatic episode. Add native mini-player/fullscreen proof for the same video node. Real providers and physical receivers remain separate acceptance.

## Sub-features

Pick channels through global search, Live TV, its guide grid, favourites or the viewer's own lists. Watch shares its live stream with the muted Home preview. Other paths include codec conversion, captions, recovery, favourite and list ordering and catch-up of programmes that aired.

## How to get to it (user POV)

After connecting, **Search** or Ctrl-K/Cmd-K finds channels and programmes. Enter starts the selected channel. **Live TV** provides channel/category and guide browsing. Its picker keeps Favourites, Recently watched and All channels beside the nine fixed browse topics, with Provider lists as the escape hatch to the original country/category hierarchy. Country, Language and Source filter the same shared descriptor in list, grid and Watch. F opens Country, Tab reaches the filters, and Up/Down/Enter/Space/Escape drive their menus. Multiple choices use OR within a dimension and AND across dimensions; facet counts apply the other filters. At 960×600, open Source after choosing Belgium and United Kingdom, then English and French, with a long saved provider name. Counts and checkmarks must stay within the viewport in all five locales, and the full label must remain accessible. Reset filters clears the session scope. The same picker is in Watch, with its active filter summary and Reset. Topic/native discovery is remembered globally; All clears it and legacy lastCategoryId. Filters reset on base changes and startup and survive a Watch return. A removed category falls back to All with a message. A list row's name and playing dot own its first line; a second line under it holds the source (only where names repeat), country and qualities, or a search group's or copy's summary, source first, at the unchanged 3.5rem row. A clipped name or line shows in full in a tooltip on hover and under the keyboard-selected row, and Escape still reaches the page. Run `pnpm verify:desktop live-names` (source `scripts/live-names.ts`): two fake providers list the same channels under the same names, one far too long. It checks both copies' lines lead with their own subscription at 3.5rem, the label on hover of the long name and none for a short one, the label while Down selects the long row, Escape leaving Live TV, no label over Watch, the same rows at 960×600 and the playing dot beside the cut name. Scripts find a row's channel by `[data-name]`, the provider's full name. A row's chevron, or Right, opens the rest of its day; on a channel with catch-up it starts with today's programmes that aired and the provider keeps, and a click plays one from its start in Watch, which says "Aired …" under the programme's title. Back in Live TV or Home, the preview goes on with the recording and says the same. Escape leaves Watch for Home; **Watch** returns to the current channel. **Guide**, beside Watch and mute in Live TV's strip, or at the end of the list's title while nothing plays, or G, shows the same list as a grid of channels by time, past included, and **List**, G or Escape goes back. Favourites are saved from channel controls and appear on Home and Live TV. **CC** lists the channel's subtitles and, under them, **Timing** while teletext, captions or HLS text shows, and the look's Size, Background and Position. **More** has no subtitle settings.

## Driving it with Electron CDP

Preconditions: `pnpm build`, a display and the fake provider's `h264-aac.mpegts` fixture. Run `pnpm verify:desktop live-tv`. Search `TEST | H.264 + AAC`, press Enter and observe Watch, an advancing video clock, nonzero picture width and decoded audio bytes. Press Escape and observe a playing, muted compact Home preview. Click Watch and observe unmuted playback, exactly one active provider connection and zero extra stream requests. Retain screenshots of the connected app, Watch, Home preview and final Watch.

For converted codecs, titles and subtitle rendering use `apps/desktop/test/e2e/packaged-app.ts`; recovery uses `live-recovery-app.ts`; favourites use `favourite-order.ts`; mapping to a source of Settings > Guides, its failure, a restart and its removal use `guide-source.ts`. Follow their documented executable arguments and capture the affected entry point, not only global search.

## Gotchas

Only the synthetic codec-test channels play media. A final frame can conceal stopped playback; require clock progress and decoded sound. Home deliberately keeps the stream running. Linux Xvfb proves functional decoding only; audible sound, GPU use, real streams and AirPlay/Cast remain separate checks.
