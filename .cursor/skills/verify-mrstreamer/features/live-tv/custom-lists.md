# Desktop custom channel lists

## Custom channel lists

The viewer's lists sit in Live TV's lists between All channels and the topics, with **New list** after them, which turns into a field in its place: Enter makes the list and selects it, Escape puts the row back, and a taken name (case and accents aside) says so in place. A row's lists button, beside its star, opens a menu of the lists, ticked where they hold the channel, with New list, which makes a list holding that channel: its field opens in the lists wherever they were, out of a Find, a country or Provider lists, while the list on screen stays, and Escape makes none. A list's header has Reorder (the Favourites draft and keys) and an options menu: Rename puts the name's field in the title's place, Move up and Move down change its place among the lists, and Delete list asks once and falls back to All channels without the removed-category notice, even when the lists are read again before the delete is answered, and after Live TV was left and opened again while it was out (the window's query client keeps its deletes, not the page); a list that goes otherwise, after a failed delete too, still shows the notice. A rename or delete answered after the viewer chose another list, or opened another field, leaves that list, its new order and its field as they are. A channel its saved subscription no longer lists shows at the list's end, dimmed, with "Not listed by … now" and Remove; one of a subscription that isn't saved counts under the filters ("1 channel is from a subscription that isn't saved."). A failed change leaves "Couldn't save." with Retry, in place for New list, rename and delete, under the filters for the menus' changes. The same list is chosen for the guide grid, Watch's channel list (channel up and down follow it) and Home's category row, and a restart keeps it.
