# Search and programme reminders

Search shows current-query channel-name matches before programme results arrive. An exact palette query with complete fresh channel, programme and title answers appears immediately from the existing query cache. Missing, expired or invalidated answers retain the typing delay; even an unobserved programme answer must pass its existing time-based freshness rule. No extra IPC is needed for the complete fresh hit. Loading remains until sources settle. Healthy accounts retain their programme matches when another fails; if all relevant guide reads fail, search reports an error instead of claiming an empty result. Editing raw text immediately makes old rows inactive, including Enter/click during debounce. Exact names rank before prefixes; aliases, structured years and apostrophe/hyphen variants retain recall. Keep the selected owned result when earlier sources prepend, preserve Automatic/copy expansion and conflicting-source grouping, and cancel only the abandoned view/request. `search-ipc.test.ts`, `library-load.test.ts`, `renderer/search-lifetime.test.ts` and the Live search/grouping suites cover those public boundaries. Cover a searched Live page with Watch, cross two polling intervals, update its guide, then return and verify current results and retained selection.

Guide search, from desktop 0.0.24 (not in Stable yet): **Search the guide**, the calendar beside the bell in Live TV's header, or the last row of ⌘K's results, replaces the channels with a search of programme titles over every day the subscription's guide keeps (`apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/features/live/GuideSearch.tsx`, `guide.searchPage`). Days (any, before today, today, tomorrow, next 7 days) and Channels (all, or the list on screen up to 100) narrow it; Subscription picks whose guide, channel ids and saved searches when several are saved. Pages of 50 are ordered by start then channel; **Show more** passes the last cursor, and a change of words or filter starts with none. Saved searches are `guide.savedQueries`/`guide.setSavedQueries`, at most 20, with named days resolved when applied and colliding shortened names numbered: a failed write keeps the list and offers Retry, and an unreadable list hides Save. Rows show S/E only from valid xmltv_ns season and episode numbers, with part absent when unspecified, otherwise an onscreen number as written, with sub-title, Shown before and Premiere; nothing says New. Each page search carries a `request` that `guide.cancelSearch` stops, like the palette's. `renderer/guide-search.test.ts` covers paging and the reset, This list's channel ids, the episode line, keys and Escape, and failed saves and reads; `search-ipc.test.ts` the cancellation. Run `pnpm verify:desktop guide-search` (with `xvfb-run -a` on headless Linux) for the maintained built-app baseline: a 240-programme past/future fake guide, header and seeded palette entry, 50-to-100 paging and filter reset, Tomorrow save/apply/restart/remove, optional episode labels, row keyboard navigation and Escape restoring the header opener. It also exercises empty Favourites and small-list apply/revisit, guide-only palette keyboard entry, future reminder persistence/removal, on-now Watch with decoded picture, and two subscriptions with distinguishable results and saved searches. The palette keeps its guide-search action available when ordinary matches are empty. In each of the five system locales at 960×600 with root text enlarged to 125% it presses ⌘K's guide row by pointer, then fails if a field or button reaches outside the window or a clipping box, or a row's painted title or channel is cut. The selected-channel strip stays hidden during search and returns when it closes; the scenario requires visible result rows in German at the same small size during playback and after Stop. There the header wraps the field below the title and rows stack the title over day, start and channel; from a 42rem list they are one-line columns. This baseline does not prove every failure/race path, native accessibility, a physical screen reader, a real provider or an installed upgrade; focused contracts and separate acceptance remain necessary.

Programme reminders: Remind me sits beside Watch live in a programme's details in the grid, under a later programme's description in the rest of a day and in Search; a second press cancels, and a set one shows a bell in its cell or at the end of its line. The bell in Live TV's header counts those still to come and lists them, with how each stands (changed, not in the guide now, missed, on now), and a cross that takes one out. Run `pnpm verify:desktop reminders` (source `scripts/reminders.ts`), on the real clock, with a fake guide of three-minute programmes. It sets one from the grid with Tab and Enter, checks the cell, the bell's count and list, sets and cancels one from a schedule line of a channel without catch-up, fails a save while `reminders.json` is a folder (the line says "Couldn't save. Try again." and the button stays) and sets it once it isn't, sets one from Search, restarts on the same profile and finds them listed, waits for the first one's start and its notice in the window, plays the channel from its Watch on one provider connection, and takes one out with the keyboard, the keys going on to the next cross. Retained: `grid-reminder-set`, `bell-list`, `schedule-reminder-set`, `save-failed`, `search-reminder-set`, `bell-after-restart`, `banner`, `bell-while-on`, `watch-from-banner` and `bell-after-keyboard-remove`, with `reminders.list` read in `proof.json`. On Linux without libnotify Electron reports no notifications, so the list's foot says the system shows none; the system notice itself, a refused permission, a click on it, sleep and resume, and lock-screen previews need native macOS, Windows and Linux checks. `reminders.test.ts`, `packages/core/test/reminders.test.ts` and the renderer's `reminders`, `guide-grid` and `live-search-grouping` hold the rest (see [testing](../../../../../docs/contributing/testing.md#programme-reminders)).
