# Screenshot assets

## Screenshot assets

The five desktop masters, the five iPhone captures and their checked capture manifest are under `docs/assets/marketing-*`. Follow `docs/contributing/marketing-artwork.md` for the licensed demo, Mac mini source capture and owned cleanup. `prepare-assets.ts` builds every window's WebP sizes, the tricks' cuts, the iPhone screens at 402 and 804 pixels wide (804 and 1608 for landscape Watching) and the social PNG, and with `--readme` the committed README poster and mark variants in `docs/assets`. Home's, Live TV's, the TV Guide's and each iPhone capture's per-entry `provenance` record their own source builds; the manifest's top-level provenance still describes the unchanged PR 208 captures. iPhone captures come from the iOS app on the simulator against `apps/ios/scripts/marketing-fixture.ts`. `marketing-capture.ts --only <names>` refreshes some captures without rewriting the others. Social links the complete Home window; watching keeps the subtitle beside its current right-hand panel. Inspect these actual assets and the rendered site at wide and 390px widths after a refresh, checking each `<picture>` source's intrinsic dimensions and descriptions. Build output stays ignored.
