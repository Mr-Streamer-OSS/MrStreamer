Programme-following rules (0.0.24): a guide programme's Record panel has **Follow programme** as a fourth Repeat choice, with Channels chips, Record as Every match (default) or New episodes only (disabled with "The guide doesn't mark new episodes here." while the guide marks none), the Start and End steppers, up to 8 matches from `recordings.previewRule` and **Turn on rule** (`recordings.saveRule`). Each airing found is a schedule naming its rule (`ScheduledRecording.rule`): its details show its standing, that the rule sets its padding, and Cancel schedule, which settles that airing; `recordings.plan` and `recordings.schedule` refuse it. Recordings lists **Rules** after Scheduled, with Edit (`RulePanel`) and Stop following (`recordings.cancelRule`) in its menu; rule-made Scheduled rows read **From a rule** and have no Edit. The bounded planner in `apps/desktop/src/main/recordings/rules.ts` is shared by preview and execution; `rules.json` keeps each airing's identity before it can start and its settlement before a deletion. The rule cases in `apps/desktop/test/recordings.test.ts` cover previews, premiere evidence, repeats, source-qualified overlapping time changes, refusal of manual edits, cancellation and write failure, and stopped or deleted airings across restart, through the main runtime and fake provider. The maintained `scripts/schedules.ts` journey creates a rule from a guide programme, checks keyboard containment and default/disabled modes, opens its generated schedule and Rules editor, renders English, Dutch, French, German and Spanish at 960 × 600 with quarter-larger text, scrolls to its actions and stops following. It then exercises the existing manual/weekday baseline. The fake guide has one airing per title; multiple matches, bounded-preview notes, proven new-only, shifted times, restart settlement and write failure use the public runtime tests rather than rendered UI. Native, real-provider and installed-release acceptance remain separate.

Rule safety checks also cover retained-history pages with current airings, the shared queue budget across edited rules, removed subscriptions, identity-less chained shifts for cancelled/deleted/active airings, Missed after restart, empty premiere tags and visible failed-write errors. Settled and active airings retain overlapping observed intervals separately from capture plans, including guide-source changes. The bounded identity history remains fail-closed and reports its full state on the rule row; unread ledgers still refuse unsafe deletions. The editor retains saved channel IDs when names cannot be read, offers Retry for channel/preview reads and lets arrow keys leave saved New episodes only when evidence disappears. Background failures are exposed by `RecordingRule.error` and the existing schedules-changed event invalidates the Rules query when failures change. Scratch renderer IPC checks exercise keyboard, failed channel-read recovery, read-only Retry and schedule-event invalidation. Exact source, observed runtime results, public screenshots and remaining limits are recorded in the ticket evidence; Linux fake-provider proof does not qualify native or real-provider behavior.

The existing Recordings navigation entry stays available while a following rule is saved, even with no matching schedules, or while its inventory is unknown or refused. A refused startup rules ledger shows localized restart guidance rather than an ineffective Rules Retry; restore access and restart to reload it. Ordinary list/schedule failures retain their own Retry. `apps/desktop/test/renderer/recording-rows.test.ts` covers rules-only, combined and later failed reads, saved-rule navigation and healthy-empty hiding through the public page and events.

Programme-following channel discovery reads up to ten guide-search pages per subscription, with up to 100 fuzzy results on each page. Only exact titles (trimmed and case-insensitive) become channel choices; repeats collapse by subscription and channel ID. Discovery returns whether any subscription still has a continuation after that budget, separately from the airing preview's limit; the editor then shows "More channels may match. The guide search limit was reached." beside the channel choices, whether or not any channel is selected. Its cache includes the search start time. The public discovery cases in `apps/desktop/test/renderer/schedule-panel.test.ts` cover a later-page exact match, the ten-page stop, subscription-qualified channel identity and at most 100 selected channels, including replacement at capacity and matching preview/save plans.
