Programme-following rules (0.0.24): a guide programme's Record panel has **Follow programme** as a fourth Repeat choice, with Channels chips, Record as Every match (default) or New episodes only (disabled with "The guide doesn't mark new episodes here." while the guide marks none), the Start and End steppers, up to 8 matches from `recordings.previewRule` and **Turn on rule** (`recordings.saveRule`). Each airing found is a schedule naming its rule (`ScheduledRecording.rule`): its details show its standing, that the rule sets its padding, and Cancel schedule, which settles that airing; `recordings.plan` and `recordings.schedule` refuse it. Recordings lists **Rules** after Scheduled, with Edit (`RulePanel`) and Stop following (`recordings.cancelRule`) in its menu; rule-made Scheduled rows read **From a rule** and have no Edit. The bounded planner in `apps/desktop/src/main/recordings/rules.ts` is shared by preview and execution; `rules.json` keeps each airing's identity before it can start and its settlement before a deletion. The rule cases in `apps/desktop/test/recordings.test.ts` cover previews, premiere evidence, repeats, source-qualified overlapping time changes, refusal of manual edits, cancellation and write failure, and stopped or deleted airings across restart, through the main runtime and fake provider. The UI journey was run once with the fake provider on Linux Xvfb, in English and German; it is not yet kept in `.cursor/skills/verify-mrstreamer/scripts/schedules.ts`, and the fake guide's one airing per title leaves the multi-airing list, the limited and left-out notes, shifted times, restart and write failure unseen in the UI. None of this qualifies a native, real-provider or installed release.
