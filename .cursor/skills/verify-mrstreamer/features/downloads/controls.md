# Desktop download controls

## Sub-features

Download a movie, a whole series or a single episode from its details, a persistent queue with one transfer at a time, pausing and resuming one download or the whole queue, moving waiting downloads up or down, the top bar's notice of how it stands, the Downloads page with queue and copies, a series' copies together by season, offline playback of a copy in the title player and going on to the next episode's copy, playback of the same subscription taking the connection, cancel, retry, delete, interrupted and replaced files, and copies kept after their subscription is removed.

## How to get to it (user POV)

A movie's details have **Download** beside Play and Save; a series' **Download** menu previews all available seasons, including Specials, its selected Play version and eligible/already-downloaded/already-queued counts. Confirm **Download … episodes** to append one file per logical episode in `seriesEpisodeOrder`, using the same `episodeFileVersion` rule as the row, with a resumed file only while listed. Complete, transferring, waiting and queued files are skipped; failed files use the existing Retry. Partial Add failures retain successful additions and name failed episodes with Retry. Changing the selected episode files, including marking a resumed episode watched or refreshing the default language, hides the old result and Retry. A late answer cannot restore that obsolete feedback; copies already queued remain in Downloads. The menu aggregates the existing statuses and offers Retry failed, Cancel the rest and Open Downloads. A version switch or unmount stops further additions after any submitted call finishes. Each episode row has a fixed icon target (`Download S1 E2`), status first in the facts line and a 3px progress bar under its still. Its cancel, retry, missing and offline icons keep the overflow control aligned. Movie and series pills reserve their widest state label in the current language, at least 200px, so Save keeps its place in every state. The control follows the download: Queued, a percentage, Waiting for playback, an error with Retry, then **Watch offline** (a movie's arrow beside it holds **Delete download**; an episode's reads **Downloaded**). **Downloads**, the last page in the top bar while any record remains (queued, running, waiting, paused, failed, complete or missing), lists the queue, then the copies on this computer with **Watch offline** and **Delete** (pressed twice). The queue goes in its order: the transfer first with **Pause** and **Cancel**, then the downloads waiting their turn, each with Up and Down (`Move <name> up`/`down`, one place among the waiting, off at either end), **Pause** or **Resume** and **Cancel**, then the failed ones with **Retry** and **Delete**. A paused one reads **Paused · <bytes> of <size>** over its still bar and keeps its place until resumed, also through a restart and playback; one whose subscription plays reads **Waiting while <subscription> plays**. The heading has **Pause all** while anything can be paused, else **Resume all** while something is. A pause, resume or move that can't be saved says **Couldn't save. Try again.** on its row, or under the heading, with its control left to press again. A row's button keeps the keyboard wherever main's list moves the row, as React gives it back while drawing the move, and where a move turns its arrow off at an end the row's other arrow takes it in that same drawing. Nothing waits for main's answer, so lists and answers may come in any order; a keyboard the viewer took to another row, or that went with a row cancelled meanwhile, is left alone. A movie's details then read **Paused · Resume**, an episode's icon resumes it (`Resume download of S1 E2`) and its facts say **Paused**; the series menu counts it as already queued, and Cancel the rest ends it. Below the width where the visible page names fit it is the download icon beside Search, with the same name and availability rule. The last deletion hides either entry without leaving an open page. Focus elsewhere stays put; a hidden entry hands focus to Home, and a normal Downloads entry that folds hands focus to its replacement. Collection changes remeasure folding; loading, errors and obsolete reads cannot claim an empty collection. While a download transfers, waits or is queued, the top bar's notice beside Search says so on every page but Downloads with its name in the bar: the arrow and a floored percentage, the bytes so far when the size is unknown (Starting before the first), **Waiting**, **Queued** or, once only paused ones are left, **Paused**, muted; **Download stopped** once a failure of a saved subscription is all that is left. Its name is "Downloads, <what it says>". Where the bar folds, it is the folded Downloads button, marked on its page. Where the other pages' names, or Back, have no room beside its words, it keeps its arrow alone and says them on hover and focus. Pressing it, with the pointer, Enter or Space, opens Downloads; over a playing title it leaves the title as Back does first, and over Watch or a title the player's keys leave Enter and Space on it to the button. With no subscription saved, Connect shows **Downloads** top right while any record remains; that page has only Downloads and **Add subscription**.

On this computer, two or more copies of episodes this build or a later one queued from the same series version of the same account go together where the newest of them is: a row with the series' poster and name, the subscription (or **Subscription removed, copy kept**), how many episodes and seasons are here and their size, and an arrow (`aria-expanded`, named after the series) that folds the episodes away in place. Under it each **Season N** or **Specials** lists its copies in the series' own order, each by its number and name with its length, **Watched** once finished, progress and time left once started, and **Watch offline** (`Watch S1 E2 offline`) and **Delete**. An episode the series lists between two copies with no copy here says `S2 E3 isn't on this computer`, or `S2 E3 to S2 E6 aren't on this computer` for a run. The series goes by what it listed as its latest download was queued (`DownloadList.series`), numbered seasons first and specials apart, or a mapped playlist's source order; copies of episodes it no longer lists come last. A series' only copy, a movie and an episode an earlier build queued stay single rows. Copies go together by series version, account and TMDB id, under a key made up for each app run that says nothing of the account; names never join them.

At the end of a copy whose series lists a next episode, the end screen counts down to that episode's copy with the **Next episode** setting and delay: `Finished S1 E2 · Offline`, `S1 E3 · <name>`, `<length> · Plays in <n>`, **Play now** and **Cancel**; once cancelled or with autoplay off it offers **Next episode** and **Downloads**, and N, media-next and the controls' **Next episode** play it too. Settings holds the countdown as online. The next copy opens only once main kept this copy's progress and main's list, read again, still has that copy complete: otherwise the end stays on this copy. A save main refuses, as while another copy of the app holds the database, keeps this copy where it is, playing or at its end, shows `Couldn't save. Try again.` and stops the countdown; nothing goes on by itself, and Next, N or the end's **Next episode** tries again. A next episode with no copy, or whose file went, says `S1 E3 isn't on this computer` and `Download it to go on.`; one still downloading says `S2 E5 is still downloading` with its status line, one failed `S2 E5 didn't download`; after the series' last episode it says `That was the last episode`; each offers **Downloads** and **Back**. Nothing skips to a later copy. A copy without a series ends as before, with **Play again**.
