# Desktop series copies and offline Next

For the prior-release executable checks, read [Desktop pause and order](queue.md).

Run `xvfb-run -a pnpm verify:desktop downloads-series` after `pnpm build` for a series' copies and going on offline (`scripts/downloads-series.ts`). It starts the built app on one profile four times, five with `--previous`, with the fake provider, fake TMDB, a loopback picture server and `network-hook.cjs`, the window resolving no host but loopback. It connects, saves a 4-second next-episode delay in Settings, opens Formats, picks its two-season Dutch version through Versions and downloads S1 E2, S1 E3 and S2 E2 from their rows. The provider, TMDB and pictures then stop. With the subscription still saved the Downloads page must show one series of three episodes, two seasons and `S2 E1 isn't on this computer`; with autoplay off S1 E2's end offers S1 E3 without a countdown and its **Next episode** plays it; with autoplay on, Settings opened over the countdown holds its seconds, and closing it plays S1 E3. The subscription is removed through Settings. With none saved: S1 E2's countdown opens S1 E3, whose end stops at `S2 E1 isn't on this computer` though S2 E2 is here, main keeping S1 E3's own progress; **Cancel** keeps S1 E2 at its end with its progress, then **Next episode** plays S1 E3; N two seconds into S1 E2 saves its progress there before S1 E3 opens; and S1 E3's file removed from the run's profile during S1 E2's countdown stops it at `S1 E3 isn't on this computer`, S1 E2 still on screen. Before that, the run holds the profile database's write lock from another connection (`begin immediate`) past main's one-second wait: N two seconds into S1 E2 must keep S1 E2 playing, say `Couldn't save. Try again.` and leave main's list and the stored record at the progress before; S1 E2's end must count down, stop when its save is refused and stay on `Finished S1 E2` past the delay. With the lock let go, N and the end's **Next episode** each play S1 E3, main having kept S1 E2's progress first. A last start must list S1 E3's file as gone and S1 E2's progress, and play S2 E2 to `That was the last episode`. Every offline playback window must show no main connection and no window request beyond the loopback proxy, `mrstreamer:`, `data:`, `blob:` and `file:`. Episodes are found by the provider's file ids in main's list, never by names.

With `--previous <executable>`, 0.0.19's unpacked AppImage (checked as for `downloads-queue`) starts after the first start, S2 E2 held there mid-transfer: it finishes S2 E2, keeps S1 E2's progress from **Watch offline** and downloads S1 E1 itself from its row. This build must then find S1 E2, S1 E3 and S2 E2 still one series, its series kept through 0.0.19's transfer, completion and progress writes, and 0.0.19's S1 E1 a single row with no series whose end offers **Play again** and nothing next. 0.0.19 itself lists every copy alone and goes on to none: that is the limit of going back, and copies it makes stay single after the upgrade. Proof is in `.local/verification/downloads-series-<unique>/`, or `downloads-series-previous-<unique>/`: `proof.json` (`ids`, `online`, `previous` with whether 0.0.19's record declares a series, `previousStart`, `providerStopped`, `autoplayOff`, `settingsHold`, `standalone`, `countdown`, `continued`, `stoppedAtGap`, `cancelled`, `nextMidPlay`, `checkpointRefusedMidPlay`, `checkpointKeptMidPlay`, `checkpointRefusedAtEnd`, `checkpointKeptAtEnd`, `missingNext`, `legacyEnd`, `offlinePlayback`, `restart`, `restartPlayback`, `mainNetwork`), screenshots with accessibility trees, `main-network.jsonl` and `electron.log`. Grouping, each season run's key, the next copy and its stops are pure rules in `packages/core/test/download-series.test.ts`; the player's countdown, hold, cancel, save-before-open, refused save, re-read and missing-file cases are renderer contracts in `apps/desktop/test/renderer/copy-next.test.ts`, the page's, with a later copy's pressed Delete and keyboard kept as earlier copies come and go, in `apps/desktop/test/renderer/downloads.test.ts`; capture, keys by version, account and TMDB id, restart and unreadable series are service contracts in `downloads.test.ts`.
