# Copied-video recovery and codecs

For the five download steps referenced below, read [Desktop downloads baseline](desktop.md).

For copied-video recovery, follow the [Titles compatibility recipe](../titles/playback.md#title-playback-recovery-and-receivers). Its offline run closes the provider, opens the downloaded copy, checks conversion and seek/track progress, and requires zero new provider file requests. This source-build check does not establish installed native acceptance.

For new-codec acceptance from desktop 0.0.16, repeat the flow with 10-bit VP9 and AV1 copies of both `title-h264-eac3-subs.mkv` (the two-sound-track movie) and `title-long-subs.mkv`. Generate synthetic Matroska copies with system FFmpeg, `-map 0 -c copy -c:v libvpx-vp9 -pix_fmt yuv420p10le` or `-map 0 -c copy -c:v libaom-av1 -pix_fmt yuv420p10le`, preserving every sound and subtitle track. In the run's isolated fake provider, `replaceMovieFile` must replace both `TEST | Two sound tracks` and `TEST | Long subtitles` before download; changing only the short movie does not verify new-codec subtitles, saved-position resume or playback after subscription removal. Follow the five steps above, include a held pause and resumed picture/audio progress, and capture the long movie after removal. Require no provider requests during offline playback. `apps/desktop/test/e2e/installed-media.ts` implements the installed AV1 flow with the candidate's bundled tools, pinned identities and per-window video-conversion headers. Its stopped-source proof remains separate from the Windows upgrade's audited network block, and requires an actual native run. The committed `title-av1-10bit.mkv` and `title-long-av1-10bit.mkv` are the exact accepted synthetic copies of the two originals above, made with FFmpeg 6.1, `libaom-av1`, `yuv420p10le` and the other streams copied. The exact encoder rate options were not recorded. Their bytes and SHA-256 are pinned in the driver; regeneration changes those bytes. The long file has one FLAC sound track; alternate sound is checked on the short file.
