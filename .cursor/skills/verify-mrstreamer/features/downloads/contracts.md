# Desktop download service contracts

Startup recovery and account-removal cases in `apps/desktop/test/downloads.test.ts` cover a refused completion save without losing completed bytes or copy identity, an unreadable inventory refusing removal until repair and restart, and unfinished-download cleanup refusing removal while keeping the account available for retry. A cleanup retry can succeed in the same run; a startup inventory or recovery failure requires a healthy restart. Complete copies remain after successful account removal. Late-admission coverage checks a `no-subscription` refusal, but does not establish every scheduling interleaving at the final synchronous publication check.
