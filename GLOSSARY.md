# Mr. Streamer

Terms for watching, organizing a personal library and keeping programmes for later.

## Language

**Recording**:
A local copy captured from a live channel by Mr. Streamer.

**Catch-up**:
An earlier programme made available to play by the viewer's provider, within that provider's retained schedule.

**Programme reminder**:
An alert for a scheduled programme. A reminder does not itself record or play the programme.

**Viewing profile**:
A person's viewing space on a shared installation, with their own progress, favourites, collections, recommendations and viewing preferences.

**Subscription**:
A connection to an external IPTV provider through a provider login or playlist.

**Live buffer**:
Temporary history of a live channel received while it is open, available for pausing and rewinding.

**Multiview**:
Several live channels viewed together, with one channel audible at a time.

**Programme-following rule**:
A recording rule that finds future broadcasts of a named programme on selected channels, including when its broadcast time changes.

**Collection**:
A named group of movies and series selected by the viewer.

**Smart collection**:
A named group of movies and series selected by saved filtering rules as the available catalogue changes.

**Followed series**:
A series from one source that a viewer asked to hear about when new episodes are listed. Following does not save it to the watchlist.

**New-episode alert**:
A notice in the app that a followed series lists episodes the viewer has not been told of yet. It lasts until the viewer has seen it.

**Recording retention rule**:
An optional policy that removes eligible recordings according to their age or how many are kept, while preserving protected recordings.

**Background operation**:
Mr. Streamer running without its main window so downloads or recordings can continue.

**Broadcast occurrence**:
One airing of a programme on a specific channel at a scheduled start time.

**Episode identity**:
Source-supplied information that distinguishes a particular episode from other episodes of the same programme. It does not by itself establish that a broadcast is a first run.

**Recording protection**:
A viewer’s mark that excludes a recording from automatic retention removal.
